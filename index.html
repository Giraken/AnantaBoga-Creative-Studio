[ 01 — About ]

Est. Kota Batu

We are a creative production studio from Kota Batu. We turn festivals, brands and communities into films people remember, from the first plan to the final cut.

Every project starts with a conversation about what the film is for. We plan the shoot with you, film on the day with a small crew and handle the edit, music and titles, so you get a film that is ready to share.
