Key features

Key features

Crafting visual stories that captivate

Anantaboga Creative Studio is a video and photo studio in Mojorejo, Kota Batu. We film the festivals, ceremonies, offices and classrooms of East Java, and cut them into films people want to watch and share.

  • From plan to final cut

    We plan the shoot with you, film on the day and handle the edit, music and titles.

  • Filmed where it happens

    On location across Batu, Malang and East Java, from village carnivals to ministry events.

  • Cut for where it's shown

    A five-minute after movie, a one-minute ad or a short for social, edited to fit.

Our services

Our services

We provide video and photo for every occasion

  • After movies Festivals, carnivals, ceremonies and company events, cut into a film your guests will share.
  • Company profiles Who you are, what you do and the people behind it, filmed on site.
  • Public service ads Short, clear films for government programs and campaigns.
  • Documentaries Longer stories about a community or a milestone, told by the people who lived it.
  • School films Class videos, ceremonies and art shows, so every student has the year on film.
  • Photography Pre-wedding sessions and product photos for local brands.
